# Mission

**Review by:** 2027-01-25

**Status:** Proposed

Give one owner, and the people and agents working with them, a platform that:

1. keeps knowledge in a form humans and agents can both query;
2. makes every decision traceable to a recorded reason;
3. protects data by design, not by later patching;
4. adds capability only when it earns its operating cost.

## Measures of mission fulfilment

- Percentage of running services with a catalogue entry and a runbook.
- Percentage of architectural decisions recorded as ADRs.
- Mean time from a documented risk to its recorded treatment.
- Restore tests passed in the last quarter.
