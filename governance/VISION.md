# Vision

**Status:** Proposed · **Owner:** Rui Silva (`9dot5`) · **Review:** every 12 months

A personal and team platform where knowledge, governance, security, AI and automation
live in one documented system. Every component exists because it produces a measurable
return, and every component can be understood, operated and removed by its owner.

## What it is

- An operating platform for humans, AI agents, MCP servers and clients.
- A set of documented decisions that outlive any single technology choice.

## What it is not

- Not a server, and not a collection of services.
- Not a homelab that grows by accumulation.
- Not an installation of a particular AI front-end.

## Horizon

- **Year 1:** governance, knowledge and a management portal running on one host, with
  backups tested.
- **Years 2–3:** AI and automation components added only where the ROI matrix supports
  them, with agents operating under documented policy.
- **Beyond:** the platform can run on another host or cloud provider without rewriting
  its governance. That is the test of portability.

## Success looks like

A new contributor, human or agent, can answer "what is this, why is it built this way,
and what is allowed to change" from the repository alone, in under 30 minutes.
