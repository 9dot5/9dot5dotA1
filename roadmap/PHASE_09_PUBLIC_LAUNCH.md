# Phase 09 — Public Launch

**Status:** Proposed · **Duration:** to be sized · **Gate owner:** Rui Silva (`9dot5`)

## Objective

Open the platform's documentation and selected components publicly, once the governance,
security and operations foundations have been tested in private use.

## Preconditions

- Phases 00–08 accepted at their gates.
- Licence chosen and applied (LICENSE, ADR-0006).
- Security review of anything published, including history scrubbed of secrets.
- Trademark and naming checked: no use of third-party names or logos without permission.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Public repository scope decided (documents only, or documents plus code)
- [ ] Release notes and versioning scheme
- [ ] Contribution guidelines reviewed for open participation
- [ ] Community and support channel defined, with a security reporting route

## Exit criteria

- Owner sign-off recorded.
- Post-launch review scheduled at 30 days.
